Questions

Good to know

Do you take on one-off projects, or only ongoing programs?

Both. Some clients need a single problem solved, such as a scanner rollout or a stalled remediation effort. Others want a program run over time. We scope to whichever fits.

How long is a typical engagement?

It depends on the goal. A focused assessment can wrap in a few weeks, while a full program build and handoff usually runs a few months. We agree on the shape before we start.

Which security tools do you work with?

Scanning with Tenable, Qualys, and Rapid7, patch and asset operations, and the automation around them in PowerShell and Python. We meet you in the stack you already own.

Are you fully remote?

Yes. We work remotely with clients nationwide, and we are comfortable operating inside your change control and access requirements.

How do you price your work?

By the engagement, not by the hour where we can help it. After the discovery call you get a clear scope and a fixed price or a defined retainer, so there are no surprises.
