About

Three decades in the trenches of enterprise security.

CloudKindness is a boutique team of security and infrastructure practitioners with a combined three decades across vulnerability management, cloud, virtualization, and data protection. The work spans enterprise vulnerability management programs covering thousands of servers and workstations across dozens of domains, sustaining a service level of 0.04 exploitable vulnerabilities per node.

That work pairs deep automation with data analysis: PowerShell and Python tooling, Power BI reporting, and exploitation-aware prioritization using EPSS, CISA KEV, and the NIST LEV model. It also reflects early adoption of large language models for security work, including an internal copilot built for triage, prioritization, and reporting.

Earlier chapters include architecture and consulting roles at EMC, VMware, and Hitachi, including a twenty million dollar backup modernization for a global bank. Cloud Kindness brings that same senior, hands-on judgment to a small number of clients at a time.
